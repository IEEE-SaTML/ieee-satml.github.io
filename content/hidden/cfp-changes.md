Changes to the [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/) and the [submission checklist](/call-for-papers/checklist/) since first publication, with new text <ins>highlighted</ins>. Editorial and stylistic edits are not listed, and the [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/) takes precedence.

## Call for Papers, 4 September 2026

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Submission information &rarr; Previous reviews</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
For papers that were previously rejected from another conference, authors <em>must append prior reviews</em> to their submission along with a description of how those reviews were addressed in the submission. […]
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
<ins>If a paper was previously submitted to another venue and received reviews</ins>, authors <em>must append those prior reviews</em> to their submission <ins>(regardless of whether the paper was rejected or withdrawn by the authors)</ins>, along with a description of how the reviews were addressed in the <ins>SaTML</ins> submission. <ins>The reviews must be placed at the very end of the paper, after all appendices.</ins> […]
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Planning for growth &rarr; Author reviewing</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
Requiring authors of submitted papers to contribute to reviewing up to three papers, as is increasingly common at venues of large size. Failure from authors to submit such reviews may result in desk rejection of their paper(s).
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
Requiring authors of submitted papers to contribute to reviewing, as is increasingly common at venues of large size. <ins>At abstract registration, every paper must nominate one of its authors who is qualified to review at SaTML and can take on this assignment. The load is cumulative: a nominated author is assigned up to three papers for each submission they are nominated on, so an author nominated on two submissions may be assigned up to six papers. Reviews that are not submitted, are late, or are of inadequate quality may result in desk rejection of any paper the author was nominated on.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Conflicts of Interest policy</p>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Added</span>
<ins>Conflicts must be declared by the abstract registration deadline, and updated in the last 24 hours before the paper submission deadline, since program-committee members may be added in the meantime.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Planning for growth &rarr; Expanding the Program Committee</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
If we do, we may ask authors to re-check their conflicts of interest against the updated committee, no later than 24 hours before the submission deadline.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
If we do, <ins>the updated committee will be in place no later than 24 hours before the submission deadline, and authors should re-check their conflicts of interest against it</ins>.
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Submission categories &rarr; "New Insights" HotCRP section</p>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Added</span>
<ins>This field is mandatory in HotCRP and cannot be left blank, so research papers should simply enter "N/A".</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Important dates &rarr; Abstract registration</p>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Added</span>
<ins>Adding or removing authors after the abstract registration deadline is grounds for desk rejection.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Submission information &rarr; Submission site</p>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
Submissions are handled through HotCRP: <a href="https://satml27.hotcrp.com">https://satml27.hotcrp.com</a> <span style="color:#7a7a7a">(previously "Submission Link TBC")</span>
</div>
</div>

## Submission checklist, 4 September 2026

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Before writing &rarr; Category</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
SoK and Position also need a 300-word "new insights" field in HotCRP.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
SoK and Position also need a 300-word "New Insights" field in HotCRP<ins>; the field is mandatory, so research papers enter "N/A"</ins>.
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Abstract registration</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
No simultaneous active abstract registration elsewhere. If under review elsewhere, that must be resolved by 29 Sep.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
<ins>Answer the HotCRP field on whether the paper is still under review elsewhere. If it is, that other submission must be resolved (rejected or withdrawn) by the paper submission deadline.</ins><br>
<ins>Nominate one author, by email exactly as listed, as author-reviewer.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Conflicts of interest</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
<em>Paper submission:</em> Declare all mandatory conflicts (see "Conflicts of Interest policy"). Fake conflicts are not allowed.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
<em>Abstract registration:</em> Declare all mandatory conflicts (see "Conflicts of Interest policy"). Fake conflicts are not allowed.<br>
<em>Paper submission:</em> <ins>Update your conflict declarations in the last 24 hours before the submission deadline: PC members may have been added since abstract registration.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Paper submission &rarr; Prior reviews</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
If rejected elsewhere, append those reviews unedited plus your response.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
If the paper <ins>received reviews at another venue, whether rejected or withdrawn</ins>, append those reviews unedited plus your response, <ins>at the very end of the paper after all appendices</ins>.
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Contingency measures</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
Be ready to review up to three papers if that requirement is activated.
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
Be ready to review up to three papers <ins>per submission you are nominated on</ins>, if that requirement is activated. <ins>The load is cumulative across submissions.</ins>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cfp-change">
<p class="cfp-where">Desk rejection</p>

<div class="cfp-quote">
<span class="cfp-label">Before</span>
missing prior reviews from an earlier submission
</div>

<div class="cfp-quote is-now">
<span class="cfp-label">Now</span>
missing prior reviews from a paper that <ins>received reviews at another venue</ins><br>
<ins>adding or removing authors after abstract registration</ins><br>
<ins>late or inadequate-quality reviews from the nominated author-reviewer, if author-reviewing is activated</ins>
</div>
</div>
