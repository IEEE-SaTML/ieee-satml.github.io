<p class="news-date">Last updated: Sep 29, 2026 &mdash; see <a href="/call-for-papers/changes/">what changed</a>.</p>

Authors should read and comply with the full [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/). We have drafted a brief checklist below to help you double-check, but it may not capture every detail, so the full CfP takes precedence. If you think we have missed something important from the CfP in the checklist, we welcome your feedback at [pcchairs@satml.org](mailto:pcchairs@satml.org).

<strong>Every item below is required</strong>, except the few marked <span class="badge badge-optional">Optional</span>. Those marked <span class="badge badge-applicable">Required if applicable</span> are required whenever the stated condition holds, and those marked <span class="badge badge-all-authors">All authors</span> must be carried out by each author individually in HotCRP. All authors remain responsible for the accuracy of the whole submission. Items are grouped by where they belong: the paper PDF, the HotCRP submission form, or an email to the Program Chairs.

## Before writing

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Scope:** safety/trustworthiness engaged throughout, not just the intro. Ask [pcchairs@satml.org](mailto:pcchairs@satml.org) if unsure.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Category and length:** Research 12pp body text; SoK 12pp body text, title starts "SoK:"; Position 5 to 12pp body text, title starts "Position:". References and appendices unlimited.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Format:** PDF, `\documentclass[conference]{IEEEtran}`, 10pt, no change in geometry/template.

## By the abstract registration deadline <span class="due-chip">Tue 22 Sep 2026, AoE</span>

🖥️ Everything below is in HotCRP; the paper itself is not due yet.

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Title, abstract, authors, affiliations, topics:** tentative title, non-blank abstract, final authors, final affiliations (no changes afterwards, not even in the camera-ready version in case of acceptance; see [Change of affiliation](/call-for-papers/#change-of-affiliation) for exceptions), final topics.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Under review elsewhere:** answer the HotCRP field. If the paper is under review elsewhere, that other submission must be resolved (rejected or withdrawn) by the paper submission deadline.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Author-reviewer nomination:** nominate one author, by email exactly as listed. The nomination cannot be changed afterwards.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> <span class="badge badge-all-authors">All authors</span> **ORCID:** in every author's HotCRP profile. This is not completed while this warning shows in HotCRP:<span class="hotcrp-warning">Authors (by abstract registration deadline): Some authors haven't added an ORCID iD to their profiles</span>

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> <span class="badge badge-all-authors">All authors</span> **Conflicts of interest:** declare all mandatory conflicts (see "Conflicts of Interest policy"). Fake conflicts are not allowed.

::: callout

### 📄 Sections in the paper (PDF)

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **"Open Science" section:** right before the references, describing what you release, or why you cannot (see [Open Science](/call-for-papers/#open-science)). Required for every category, including Position papers.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-applicable">Required if applicable</span> **"LLM usage considerations" section:** after Open Science and before the references, if any LLM was used. It must meet all the criteria in [Usage of LLMs](/call-for-papers/#usage-of-llms), including the required editorial-use statement.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-applicable">Required if applicable</span> **"Ethical Considerations" section:** before the references, if the work poses ethical risks worth discussing (see [Ethical Considerations](/call-for-papers/#ethical-considerations)).
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-applicable">Required if applicable</span> **Prior reviews:** if the paper received reviews at another venue (e.g., rejected, withdrawn, or accepted in non-archival venues), append the most recent set of reviews received, unedited, at the very end of the paper after all appendices, together with a description of how they were addressed and what changed in the paper since then.<span class="checklist-note"><span class="badge badge-optional">Optional</span> You may also include the rebuttal or discussion messages, the name of the venue, and the reviews from earlier submissions if the paper was submitted more than once. The most recent set of reviews received remains compulsory to attach unedited and discuss.</span>  

The Open Science, LLM usage considerations, and Ethical Considerations sections, and any appended prior reviews, do not count towards the page limit.

### 📄 The paper as a whole

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Anonymized paper:** no names or affiliations, own work cited in third person, no hints that artifacts are already public, all linked material anonymous. Keep essential background citations.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Citations correctness:** verify every reference and result yourself. Fabrications count as misconduct. Self-check with [hallucinator.science](https://hallucinator.science/).  

### 🖥️ In HotCRP

- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **All compulsory fields:** in the submission form, following the Call for Papers and the description of each field in HotCRP.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Anonymous repository link:** e.g. [anonymous.4open.science](https://anonymous.4open.science/). If the paper has no code, write "See Open Science section": every paper needs that section, even a purely theoretical one.<span class="checklist-warning">⚠️ These links have a default expiry window: authors are required to **set the expiry date to at least 1 March 2027**.</span>  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **"New Insights" field:** SoK and Position papers summarise their insights in 300 words; research papers enter "N/A" (see [New Insights](/call-for-papers/#new-insights)).  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> <span class="badge badge-all-authors">All authors</span> **Conflict declarations:** every author must have declared their conflicts by the submission deadline. The Program Committee was finalized on 28 September 2026, so check them against the final [committee](/organization/) before submitting.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> <span class="badge badge-all-authors">All authors</span> **Explanation of any PC Conflict marked as "Other" or "Personal" (one per line):** fill in this HotCRP field.  
- [ ] <span class="badge badge-required">Required</span> **Mark the submission as "Ready for review"**, and save it. A submission left as a draft is not submitted, and submitting confirms the paper does not overlap with published, accepted, or concurrently submitted work.

## Desk rejection

The grounds for desk rejection are set out in the relevant sections of the [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/). In summary, a paper may be desk rejected for one or more of the following reasons:

* broken anonymity, or self-promotion that reveals author identity  
* incorrect format or excessive length  
* work that is out of scope, or clearly below the quality bar (after initial review by program and area chairs)  
* violations of the Open Science or LLM policies, including non-existent references  
* missing author ORCIDs or author certification  
* missing prior reviews from a paper that received reviews at another venue  
* adding or removing authors or affiliations after abstract registration  
* late or inadequate-quality reviews from the nominated author-reviewer, if author-reviewing is activated

This summary may not capture every detail, and the full CfP takes precedence.
